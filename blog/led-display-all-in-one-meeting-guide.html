A meeting room has traditionally used a projector or a large TV. The all-in-one LED screen is a newer option: a large display with the electronics and the audio built in, sized to the room. It suits a space where a seamless large image matters.

This led display conference all in one guide is written for buyers of meeting room displays. It covers how the screens work, the features, the sizes, and the selection.

How Does an All-in-One Screen Work?

The all-in-one screen has the LED panels, the controller, the power, and the audio integrated into a unit, sometimes with the camera and the conferencing system. The unit is assembled and connected like a large display, without the separate components of a video wall.
