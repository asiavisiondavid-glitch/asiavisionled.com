Running the Checklist on a North American Project

On a US or Canadian project the checklist carries additional steps that do not appear on a general purchase, because the installation is usually documented for a third party who was not involved in the purchase.

Four steps belong on the list. Confirm the contracting entity matches the name on the purchase order and the invoice, since a mismatch delays payment rather than the delivery. Collect the insurance certificate before installation starts, because on many US projects the general contractor will not allow equipment onto site without it. Confirm who is the importer of record for the entry, because the duty liability and the record-keeping obligation follow that party. And agree who witnesses the acceptance test, since the result feeds a payment application rather than an internal quality record.

Where the project is contract-governed, add the manufacturing-origin check to the list. That requirement is verified from documentation at the specification stage, not after the award.
