Spare Parts Logistics for US Service

Spare parts logistics in North America is shaped by the fact that the service obligation usually carries a response time measured in working days, which an overseas stock position cannot meet.

Three arrangements are used. Local stock held by the integrator, sized to the number of modules in service. A critical-spares kit on site, covering the components whose failure stops the wall — receiving cards and power supplies before modules. And a stock held at a third-party logistics provider in the region, which supports several sites and is normally the most economical arrangement once a fleet is large enough.

Two practical points. The spares must match the installation, so the module batch and firmware version should be recorded and the spares drawn from the same batch where possible. And where a part is replaced under warranty, the freight allocation should be agreed in advance, since on a single module it can exceed the part value.
