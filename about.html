Company Profile

Founded in 2015 in Shenzhen — the global hub of LED manufacturing — Asia Vision Technology Co., Ltd. has grown into a trusted provider of professional LED display solutions.

We handle R&D, production, sales and service across the full range: indoor and outdoor LED screens, fine pitch displays, rental screens, transparent LED displays, LED posters, floor tiles and interactive touch all-in-one machines.

With a 5,000㎡ production base and products exported worldwide, we combine manufacturing expertise with customer-focused service.

Standards We Ship On

Every cabinet is produced and assembled in our own plant rather than sourced from trading stock. That means the cabinet size, pixel pitch and finish are specified to your project — and every batch is burn-in tested while the panels are still in the factory.

Boards and panels are measured and matched across a batch, so a screen assembled from the same order reads as one surface on site. Spares and brackets stay interchangeable between production runs.

CE · RoHS · FCC · ISO 9001 certified. OEM and ODM supported, including cabinet, structure, configuration and branding.
