SMT Production Line

High-Speed Automated Assembly

Chip-on-Board Precision Manufacturing

  • Class 10,000 clean room environment
  • Flip-chip bonding technology

  • Vibration & drop testing

Quality Control

  • In-process QC at every station (IPQC)
  • Final QC before packing (OQC)
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management system
