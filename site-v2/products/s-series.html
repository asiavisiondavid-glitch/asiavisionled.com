Rental LED Display — S Series

Universal Rental · Indoor & Outdoor · Competitive

S Series is a competitive and user-friendly rental LED display. Indoor (P1.95-P3.91) and outdoor (P2.97-P4.81) models with unified 500×500mm/1000mm cabinet platform. SMD 1R1G1B technology. Perfect for events, stages, and churches — one system for all scenarios.

Where the S Series Fits

S Series is the workhorse rental platform: a straight rental screen where the project needs a dependable cabinet, quick assembly and a sensible price, rather than a specialised attribute. Events, stages, churches and temporary installations are the usual applications.

The same 500×500mm and 500×1000mm cabinet platform covers both the indoor and outdoor versions, so a rental fleet can standardise on one rigging and spare-parts system across both.

Indoor or Outdoor, and Which Pitch

The outdoor version (P2.97–P4.81) is built for daylight and weather; the indoor version (P1.95–P3.91) is built for controlled light and closer audiences. Choosing an outdoor panel for an indoor event adds weight and cost for brightness that will never be used.

Set the pitch from the nearest audience position. For a stage, that is the front row rather than the back of the hall.

Questions Buyers Ask

Can indoor and outdoor cabinets share rigging?

Where they share the same cabinet platform, yes — that is the reason the series exists. Confirm the specific option when ordering, because the locking and power details can differ between indoor and outdoor builds.

What should be confirmed before the order?

The nearest viewing distance, indoor or outdoor use, the rigging method, and whether curved assembly is required. We will confirm the panel and issue the drawings.
