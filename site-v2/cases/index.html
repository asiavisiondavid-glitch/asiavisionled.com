Le Mans 24 Hours — Dual-Side Giant LED Screens

🇫🅯 Le Mans, France

The 24 Hours of Le Mans is one of the harshest environments an LED display can face. Partner LED Visual Innovation deployed two outdoor screens for the 2026 event, with Asia Vision Technology providing manufacturing support: a trackside main screen carrying live race standings and video feeds, and a movable trailer screen serving the fan zone with event information and sponsor content. Both had to stay readable in direct daylight, seal against rain and dust, resist trackside vibration, and hold their colour and brightness steady across a full day and night of continuous operation.

Broadcast

Yle News Finland — Official Broadcast Studio Rebrand

🇫🇮 Helsinki, Finland

Finland's national broadcaster Yle (Yle Uutiset) launched a bold visual rebrand in June 2024. Purple now defines news programming while lime green marks sports — letting viewers instantly distinguish content at a glance. The centrepiece is a custom panoramic ultra-wide curved LED display that wraps the studio: purple gradients and solid colour blocks flow across the backdrop, ring lighting and anchor desk, holding brand consistency across live broadcast and on-demand streaming. The seamless curved surface reproduces colour faithfully without the green/blue spill that plagues chroma-key setups — purple complements skin tones rather than competing with them.

Virtual Production

17m × 4m XR Virtual Production LED Wall — J-Shaped Stage

🇺🇸 United States (East Coast)

An XR virtual production stage built around a 17m × 4m J-shaped LED wall, with rigid and flexible P1.95 panels spliced into one continuous curved surface and an integrated ceiling. The wall is tuned for Unreal Engine-based in-camera VFX, with HDR grading and dark-scene optimisation preserving detail from highlight to shadow. Camera-to-LED shutter sync keeps the wall frame-locked for clean capture, and the seamless join between physical set and digital environment extends the usable stage beyond its physical footprint.

SAP Garden Munich — Fire-Rated LED Display in Evacuation Zones

🇩🇪 Munich, Germany

Munich's newest state-of-the-art multi-purpose arena, inside the historic Olympic Park, has been equipped with Asia Vision CreativeLine + SFP series LED displays since September 2024. Five custom screens totalling approximately 50 square metres are installed across four critical evacuation zones — engineered to EN13501-1 / DIN4102 Class A2 fire-rated compliance in partnership with HG Videowall GmbH. Safety signage that meets European fire standards without compromising on image quality.
