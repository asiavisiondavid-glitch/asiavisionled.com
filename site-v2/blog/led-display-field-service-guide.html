Field Service in the North American Market

Field service in North America is normally contracted through an integrator rather than directly with the manufacturer, and the service obligation usually carries a response time that has been promised to an owner.

Three points follow. Spares must be locally held, because an overseas stock position cannot meet a response measured in working days. The replacement unit must match the wall, which means the module batch and firmware version have to be recorded at installation rather than guessed at the time of the fault. And the visit has to be documented, because the integrator evidences performance to the owner from a record rather than from memory.

Two practical points. Agree which components are held locally, sized to the number of modules in service. And confirm who bears the freight on a warranty replacement, since on a single-module failure the freight can exceed the part.
